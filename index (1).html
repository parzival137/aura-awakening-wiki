    Aura Awakening

    A datapack that gives players a levelling "System". Survive a trial to awaken, pick one permanent path, then grow through levels, stats, mana and skills. Four paths, one weapon, and clans.

    The Awakening Trial300 kills, the Warden, the Wither, and a brush with death. Choosing a PathShadow Monarch, Limitless, Demon Slayer or Demon. Permanent. Weapon and CastingRight-click to cast. F and Q cycle your skills. Demon SlayerThe System picks your breathing style from how you played. DemonBlood Demon Arts, Moon ranks, Moon Breathing and sunlight. ClansFound a clan so your skills and shadows never hurt your friends.

    At a glance

    • Minecraft version: 26.3 (data pack format 121).
    • Install: drop the zip in <world>/datapacks/ and run /reload. Server-side only, no mods.
    • Controls: one weapon item plus clickable chat menus and /trigger commands. Players never need operator permission.
    • Multiplayer: every player has their own level, stats, mana, skills and army.
    • Level cap: 100.
    New in v6Demon Slayer and Demon paths, clans, and a single weapon that replaces the old hotbar Combat Mode. See the version history.

    Installation

    Three steps, and it works on any world.

    1. Download the zip. Keep it zipped, or unzip it so the folder contains pack.mcmeta and data/ directly.
    2. Place it in your world. Put it in <world>/datapacks/. On a server that is the datapacks folder inside the world folder.
    3. Reload. Run /reload in game. Chat shows "Aura Awakening datapack loaded."
    VersionThe pack declares format 121 for Minecraft 26.3. Other versions may refuse to load it or break its commands.

    Updating from the old hotbar version

    Replace the zip and run /reload. Levels, stats and shadows are kept. Players who were in the old hotbar Combat Mode get their stored hotbar back automatically and receive the new weapon. Old shadows are moved onto their owner's new team.

    Uninstalling cleanly

    1. Run /function sl:system/uninstall once. It puts weapons away, removes all shadows, resets every player and removes the scoreboards and teams.
    2. Delete the datapack from the datapacks folder and run /reload.

    The Awakening Trial

    Nobody starts with the System. Players earn it by passing a three-part trial.

    Requirements

    1. Slay 300 monsters. Counted from the first time the pack sees you, using Minecraft's own mob-kill statistic.
    2. Slay the Warden. Marks the first seal.
    3. Slay the Wither. Marks the second seal.
    4. Reach the brink of death. Once all three are done, you must be at 1.5 hearts or less (3 health points).

    Check progress at any time with /trigger sl_trial. It shows kills out of 300 and whether the Warden and Wither are slain.

    What you see

    • The first time you appear in the world you get a short message that "something ancient stirs".
    • Killing the Warden and the Wither each prints a hint from "???".
    • When the kills and both bosses are done, you are told that the System only chooses those at the edge of death. Drop to 1.5 hearts or less and you awaken.

    On awakening

    • Level 1, 0 XP and 3 unspent stat points. Max mana is calculated and your mana is filled.
    • You are asked to choose your path. Nothing else is unlocked until you do.
    Before awakeningYou fight the bosses by ordinary means. No skills or shadows exist yet. Clans, however, are open to everybody from the start.

    Admins can skip the trial for a player with /execute as <player> run function sl:admin/awaken.

    Rules

    • Until you choose, skill and army commands show the path prompt instead.
    • A skill from another path gives an error and does nothing.
    • Extraction, Arise and boss shadows are Monarch-only. Only Monarchs get the extraction prompt.
    • You are handed your weapon the moment you choose. See Weapon and Casting.
    • To change path, an admin must reset you or use sl:admin/set_path (see Admin Tools).

    Stats and Levels

    Kills give XP, levels give stat points, stat points shape your power.

    Earning XP

    • Every monster kill is worth 5 XP.
    • Tougher monsters add bonus XP on top (see the extraction tiers): +5 to +20.
    • Bosses: Warden +600, Wither +500, Ender Dragon +1000.

    Levelling

    XP needed for next level = level × 10 + 20 Level 1 → 2: 30 XP Level 10 → 11: 120 XP Level 50 → 51: 520 XP Cap: level 100. Past it, XP no longer converts to levels.

    Each level gives 3 stat points (you also start with 3). A level up refills your mana, heals you and shows a title. When a level unlocks a skill, chat names it. Demons also get a message when they reach a new Moon rank.

    The five stats

    Spend points with the [+] buttons in /trigger sl_menu, or /trigger sl_stat set 1..5. Each point costs one stat point.

    #StatPer pointAlso used for
    1STR+0.4 attack damageDamage of Slayer and Demon techniques, Dagger Rush, and half of it adds to shadow damage.
    2AGI+2% movement speed
    3SENSE+0.5 luck
    4VIT+2 max health (1 heart) and +0.5 armor
    5INT+10 max mana, faster mana regenSpell damage, extraction chance, army size, boss odds.

    Mana

    Max mana = INT × 10 + level × 2 + 50 Regen = INT ÷ 4 + 2 per second (whole numbers, capped at max)

    Every path uses the same mana pool. Example: a level 30 player with INT 10 has 10 × 10 + 30 × 2 + 50 = 210 max mana and regenerates 10 ÷ 4 + 2 = 4 per second.

    Putting it away

    • /trigger sl_combat set 1 or the [Combat Mode] button puts the weapon away. Use it again to draw it.
    • The weapon comes back when you respawn. It does not drop on death.

    Cooldowns

    Each Slayer and Demon technique has its own short cooldown (see the tables), so you cannot spam one form. Monarch and Limitless skills only use mana and level checks.

    How it works under the hoodThe weapon is a carrot on a stick re-skinned as a sword, dagger or stick, because a datapack cannot detect right-click on real swords. Q and F are detected indirectly: a dropped weapon is deleted and handed back, and a swapped weapon is put back in your hand. You may see a one-tick flicker, and you cannot drop or swap the weapon on purpose.

    Who is hit

    Zombies (including villager, husk, drowned), skeletons (stray, wither skeleton, bogged), spiders, creepers, witches, illagers (pillager, vindicator, evoker, ravager), vex, phantoms, blazes, ghasts, slimes, magma cubes, silverfish, endermites, hoglins, zoglins, piglin brutes, shulkers, breezes, creakings and players who are not in your clan.

    Shadow Extraction

    After killing certain monsters you get ten seconds to try to bind their shadow to your army.

    How it works

    1. Kill a supported monster. If the prompt is on, chat offers an [Extract] button.
    2. Attempt within 10 seconds. Click it, or use /trigger sl_extract set 1. Only your latest kill can be extracted.
    3. Pay 25 mana. It is spent whether or not it succeeds.
    4. Roll. Success adds one shadow of that type to your roster. Failure: "the shadow crumbled to dust".

    Turn the automatic prompt off with /trigger sl_menu set 2. You can still extract by command during the window.

    Chance

    chance % = INT × 2 + level ÷ 2 + 45 − (tier × 10) clamped between 10% and 95%

    Example: level 30, INT 10 gives 80% for a tier 0 monster, 50% for tier 3 and 40% for a Ravager.

    Supported monsters

    TierMonstersChanceBonus XP per kill
    0Zombie, Skeleton, Spider, Cave Spider, Silverfishbase0
    1Husk, Drowned, Stray, Bogged, Creeper, Phantom, Pillager−10+5
    2Witch, Vindicator, Blaze, Hoglin−20+10
    3Wither Skeleton, Evoker, Piglin Brute, Enderman, Iron Golem−30+15

    Higher tiers cost you chance but give more XP, so a strong INT matters most. Each extraction adds one to your count for that type, and you can hold many of one type.

    Shadow Army

    Your extracted shadows fight beside you until you dismiss them.

    Arise

    • Costs 30 mana. It is the last skill on your dagger, or use /trigger sl_army set 1 or the menu button.
    • It first sends away your current shadows, then summons from your roster up to your army cap.
    • If your roster is empty, the mana is refunded and you are told to extract first.
    army cap = level ÷ 4 + INT + 3 shadow damage per hit = level ÷ 4 + STR ÷ 2 + 4
    Fill orderWhen the cap is lower than your roster, shadows are summoned in the roster's fixed order, starting with zombies, then husks, drowned, skeletons and so on. Higher-tier types come last and may not fit.

    Behaviour

    • Shadows follow you and teleport to you if they fall more than 32 blocks behind.
    • They attack hostile monsters and players outside your clan within 14 blocks, striking about every 0.6 seconds, and ignore passive mobs.
    • Each has Resistance II, Fire Resistance and Regeneration with no time limit.
    • They glow purple and are named "Shadow Zombie" and so on. They drop nothing and do not despawn.
    • They always sit on your team (your clan's team, or a personal one), so they never attack you or your clan.
    • If you leave the game, your shadows are dismissed.

    Managing the army

    • /trigger sl_army set 3 prints your roster with counts and boss buttons.
    • /trigger sl_army set 2 dismisses every shadow.

    Boss Shadows

    The Warden, the Wither and the Ender Dragon can be extracted as one-of-a-kind shadows. It is difficult and risky.

    BossRequiresExtract costSummon costOdds
    WardenLevel 60, INT 15100100INT ÷ 5 + level ÷ 10 + 3
    WitherLevel 75, INT 20150150same, minus 3
    Ender DragonLevel 90, INT 25200200same, minus 6

    Odds are clamped between 2% and 20%. For example level 60 with INT 15 gives 12% for the Warden.

    Extracting

    • If you are under the requirements when the boss dies, chat tells you what is missing and nothing is offered.
    • Otherwise you get a 10 second window with an [Attempt] button.
    • Each attempt, pass or fail, starts a 10 minute boss cooldown.
    • You can hold each boss shadow only once.
    BacklashOn failure your mana drops to 0 and you get Weakness II for 30 s, Slowness II for 15 s and Nausea for 8 s. The shadow is lost.

    Summoning

    Use the roster buttons or /trigger sl_army set 11 (Warden), 12 (Wither), 13 (Ender Dragon). Only one boss shadow can be out at a time, and summoning another replaces it.

    • Warden: melee strikes and a sonic boom at range.
    • Wither: fires skulls that inflict Wither at up to three targets.
    • Ender Dragon: flies after enemies and sweeps everything within 10 blocks.

    Boss shadows follow the same team and target rules as normal shadows and scale from your level and STR.

    Demon Slayer

    You do not pick your breathing style. The System reads how you played before you chose, and gives you the one style that fits.

    How your style is chosen

    From the moment the datapack is installed, every 5 seconds the System scores where you are and what you are doing for each of the 12 base styles. When you choose Demon Slayer, the highest score wins.

    • Where you are: oceans, rivers and swimming (Water), the Nether, deserts, badlands and fire (Flame), mountains and high air (Wind), caves and deep underground (Stone), taiga, snow and night (Mist), villages and other players (Love), swamps and jungle (Serpent), allays (Sound), forests without chest armour (Beast), flower fields (Flower), lush caves and bees (Insect).
    • What you did: vanilla statistics such as swimming, sprinting, climbing, sneaking, jumping, elytra flight, breeding, trading, note blocks, records, bells, fireworks, brewing, splash potions, bone meal, crops, stone mined, damage dealt and taken, and spiders killed.
    • Sun Breathing (Hinokami Kagura) goes to players who played evenly: at least 9 styles within half of their best score, and a best score of 200 or more.
    • No data at all: a style is rolled at random.

    You get only that one style. A chat message tells you what you were given and why. Admins can change it (see Admin Tools).

    Clans

    Clans are open to everybody, awakened or not. Your clan can never be hurt by you in any way.

    Founding a clan

    1. Rename a Name Tag. Use an anvil. The new name is your clan name, 3 to 16 characters.
    2. Hold it and open the menu. Run /trigger sl_clan set 1 and click [Create Clan]. The tag is used up.
    3. Pick a colour. Click [Colours] and choose one of 16.

    Members

    • Invite: the leader opens the menu and picks an online player without a clan. They get [Accept] and [Decline] buttons for 60 seconds.
    • Size: up to 20 members by default. Admins can change it.
    • Leave, Kick or Disband: the leader can kick and disband (disbanding asks you to confirm). Anyone can leave.
    • Succession: if the leader leaves, the longest-standing member takes over. When the last member leaves, the clan disbands.
    • Clan chat: vanilla, /teammsg <text> or /tm. Your clan name appears as a prefix.

    Clan menu triggers

    Friendly fire and PvP

    • Vanilla team friendly fire is off for clans, so melee and arrows between members do nothing.
    • Every skill, push, pull, debuff and shadow skips your clan. People outside your clan are fair game for your skills and your shadow army.
    • Without a clan, awakened players are enemies of each other.
    • Admin kill-switch: /function sl:admin/pvp {on:0} makes the pack's skills and shadows treat everyone as friendly. Vanilla melee still follows server.properties.
    LimitDamage you cause yourself with vanilla means (lava, TNT, fire you place) cannot be blocked by a datapack.

    FAQ

    Nothing happens when I use a skill.

    You have not chosen a path yet. Run /trigger sl_menu, check "Path", and click a path button. Also check that you are holding your weapon.

    I cannot see my weapon.

    You put it away. Run /trigger sl_combat set 1 to draw it. An admin can also run sl:admin/give_item.

    "Skill locked. Requires level N."

    Your level is too low. Locked skills are skipped when you cycle with F and Q.

    Can I choose my breathing style?

    No. The System picks it from how you played before you chose. Only an admin can change it with sl:admin/set_style or reroll_style.

    I am a demon and I keep burning.

    Stay under a roof, in a cave or in water, or play at night. You are immune from level 60. Admins can disable sunlight with sl:admin/sunburn {on:0}.

    My skills do not hit the Warden, Wither, Dragon or Enderman.

    They are not on the target list. This applies to your shadows too.

    Can I hurt other players?

    Yes, anyone outside your clan. Clan members are always safe from you. Without a clan, awakened players are enemies of each other. Admins can switch this off with sl:admin/pvp {on:0}.

    How do I make a clan?

    Rename a Name Tag at an anvil, hold it, and open /trigger sl_clan set 1. Details on the Clans page.

    Q or F feels odd on the weapon.

    They are detected indirectly, so you may see a one-tick flicker. You cannot drop or swap the weapon while you hold it. A shield in your off hand is returned to you.

    Do all players share levels or armies?

    No. Everything is per player.

    The pack does not load.

    It is built for Minecraft 26.3 (format 121). Check the version and that pack.mcmeta sits at the top level of the zip or folder.
